A hobbit of the Shire, born among the North-Tooks and married out of them. She grew up with her aunt
the sheriff, beating the bounds on the edges of the Shire. Her husband died recently and
unexpectedly, and she grieves him deeply. Her children are grown and settled about the Shire, and
she had taken to going further afield than a respectable hobbit ought, until one spring she crossed
the Brandywine for mushrooms and met [[tom-bombadil|Tom]] skipping stones in the Old Forest.

She is the Company's scholar and its artist. Beneath the ruins she wrote the Company's entry in the
Rangers' ledger and drew the sleeping white beast in its margin, and it was that drawing that made
Tom mark the other Banes on Perry's map. [[moriel|Moriel]] put Findemir's journal and map into her
hands,, and she carries them. At the [[mathom-house|Mathom-house]] she helped the child
[[ammie|Ammie]] carry off his grandmother's marble bust, broke her toes doing it, and took the sixth
volume of Melody Took's journals under her shirt.

She talked a troll into eating rations that she swore would let it walk in the sun, and it did, four
steps, and turned to stone with Buddy on its shoulder. At the Havens she found her way into the
kitchens, and she and Lily made the braided nutmeg bread for the Elves who came home off the
*Lómelindë*.

Her daughter [[flora-bunce|Flora]] is expecting. Marigold is going to be a grandmother.

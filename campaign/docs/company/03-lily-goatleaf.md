A woman of Bree who waited tables at the Prancing Pony, and listened. The Rangers talked more freely
with her than with most, because she was gently curious and never prying, and from them she learned
that Bree-land was not the safe place Bree-folk took it for. She never told anyone; they would not
have believed her. When the folk gathering in Bree grew stranger, and she overheard the Ranger
[[findemir|Findemir]] say he was setting out west, she felt a dire portent in it. She borrowed a
half-starved pony called Buddy and went after him to bring back help. Perry, a distant cousin of the
kind that depends on how you count, went with her.

She grew up on a farm, and all her spear-training was mock fights with her cousins in the yard. Her
first frost-wolf she killed outright, screaming "Think of it as a haystack!", and the frost left an
after-image etched into her spear-head that will not come off. She knows the ways of the Dúnedain's
way-stations, and she knows how to announce a party so as not to alarm the folk inside.

She keeps her hands busy when things are tense: shelving books at the Arantarion unasked, cleaning
the stables, calling the Company to a family meal every day at the villa in the Havens. She
nursed Buddy from half-starved to sleek; he ran in the festival's horse-run beside his friend Levlay.
At the troll's bridge Buddy threw her against the stonework and she broke a couple of ribs.

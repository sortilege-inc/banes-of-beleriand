A Dwarf of Durin's folk, a hundred and seventeen years old when the tale begins. Her people keep
the Grey Mountains, far off in the north-east beyond Mirkwood; her own kin, a sister and a sister's
family, live in the Blue Mountains. Among her clan she is the one they send for when something dead
will not stay dead: in the mountains there are always things that creep into the places where
people live, and she has a knack for finding them and putting an end to them. She went out to the
Battle of Five Armies, though not in the front line; she was needed at home, and was called out
only when she was wanted, for the dead.

Her clan sent her north with the Ranger [[findemir|Findemir]] and told her nothing of his errand. She was
alone among four frost-wolves with him down at her feet when the Company came over the rise
([[02-findemir-and-the-ruins|Findemir & the Ruins]]). She built the sledge that carried him back to
[[barad-tarminale|Barad Tarminalë]], and in the dark beneath the ruins she cleaved the ghost of the
man in the blue-and-white belt with one stroke of her axe. The Company made a song of it,
*Ghostcleavers*, and she sings it gladly.

From the dead man she took a necklace of Dwarvish make with a badger worked on it in profile, and
with it at her throat she stared down a frost-wolf. From the store beneath the ruins she took two
goblets, a ring worked with a bird, and a band of gold set with gems for her hair. In Michel Delving
she went looking for a pony of her own, with some of it to trade, and found a young chestnut called
Charlie.

She is fiercely private about names. When [[tom-bombadil|Tom]] spoke Fenja's secret name aloud she
was not pleased, and she has reminded the Company since never to say it.

At the Havens it was Már who drew [[thramli|Thramli]] and his Dwarves into the Company's counsels,
and learned from them of the [[cavity-stone|Cavity Stone]]. She brought the Company home to the
[[blue-mountains|Blue Mountains]] with [[nenuviel|Nenuviel]], and there, in a valley half a day from
the nearest hall, she called her nephew [[nolly|Nolly]] back out of a madness that had taken him
([[09-the-blue-mountains|The Blue Mountains]]). She speaks for the Company before the council of the
Dwarves.

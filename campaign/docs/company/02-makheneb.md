An Elf of Mirkwood and a Warden of that wood, young for his people at something under three hundred
years. He fought in the Battle of Five Armies. As a youth he went on an expedition into the southern
forest while the Necromancer still held Dol Guldur, and it was the worst place he had ever been.
His family fell on hard times long ago, and lately his luck had turned so far that he had lost
nearly everything he carried, down to the shirt on his back.

He came west on foot, following a feeling: not the malice of a wraith, but something alien with a
pull to it. It led him into the Old Forest, where a bear nearly had him, and [[tom-bombadil|Tom]]
sang it away ([[01-from-bree-to-toms-house|the first chapter]]). Tom's house was the heart of the
strangeness he had followed. [[goldberry|Goldberry]] lent him her horse, Strawberry, to be returned
within the year, and the others have envied him her ever since.

He is the Company's archer and its healer. He took the wolf off Perry with one shot and the great
wolf off Perry's arm with another, and he tastes evil the way others taste spice: he broke and
burned a wolf-blooded arrow that felt worse to him than Dol Guldur, and said nothing, so as not to
frighten the others. In the foothills he laid a dead explorer to rest in the way of his people, and
took her helm and shield. Beneath the ruins he took a great bow of Númenórean make from the store;
it might be the Great Bow of Angnir. It carried a shadow, and the smiths of the Havens cleansed it
for him, though something of the shadow lingers and draws his enemies' notice.

At the Havens, when two Elves spoke of the Battle of Five Armies as a little blip in the east, he
named the dead he had known who fell there, one after another, louder and angrier. "You weren't
there." Word got round, and the Elves gave him room.

---
name: The frost-wolves
side: foes
first: Four out of the south, spring 2965
---

Dire wolves, white-furred and huge, with the hoar-frost of winter about them in the spring. One came
into the Old Forest and Tom kicked it out. Four brought down Findemir; one reformed itself out of ice
in the outer chamber, and Makheneb burned it from the inside with a fire-arrow.

name: Thramli
side: allies
first: The Dwarven House, summer 2965
---

A Dwarf of the Blue Mountains who came to the Havens with his Dwarves to petition Círdan, and waited
a long while for an audience. He told Már of the [[cavity-stone|Cavity Stone]] and what it was doing
to the minds of those who kept it. His party rode home ahead of the Company, and the Company found
all five dead in the valley below the mountains ([[09-the-blue-mountains|The Blue Mountains]]).

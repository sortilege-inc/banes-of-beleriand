A young woman of the long-folk of the [[tree-of-sorrow|Tree of Sorrow]], hardly more than a girl,
with brown hair in tight ringlets and the dark, leathery skin of her people. She learned the
Company's names, played Marigold's recorder, and took them into the tree. With gestures the
long-folk told them that *she* who sleeps beneath the roots had last woken a couple of generations
ago, and that they had put her back to sleep at great cost ([[05-beneath-the-tree-of-sorrow|Beneath
the Tree of Sorrow]]).

---
name: The sleeper beneath the Tree
side: foes
first: The grotto, early summer 2965
---

A bald woman, not of the long-folk, something like an Elf and like nothing Marigold had seen, held
asleep by the roots in a grotto beneath the [[tree-of-sorrow|Tree of Sorrow]]. The long-folk's mime
was of something that leaps on your back and bites. She last woke a couple of generations ago, and
the long-folk put her back to sleep at great cost.

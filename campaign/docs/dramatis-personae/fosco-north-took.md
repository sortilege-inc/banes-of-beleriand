---
name: Fosco North-Took
side: allies
first: Trappers, early summer 2965
---

A hobbit of Long Cleeve and Marigold's second cousin once removed. Something had been robbing his
traps for six months, and the Company found out what: an old Orc out of the Moors. He scowled at the
Dwarf and the Elf, and called them Mari and Mackie. "Little old Marigold, gone so far, and in such
company."

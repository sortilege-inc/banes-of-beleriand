---
name: Otto
side: allies
first: Haelond, summer 2965
---

An old Elf who catches crabs with his hands on the flats of Haelond at low tide. He was a sailor
once, is not quite ready to go west, and enjoys the waiting, and the cooking. He cured Perry of his
fear of the sea, and taught him that the Third Age is an age of loss.

---
name: The Orc of the Moors
side: foes
first: Long Cleeve, early summer 2965
---

An old Orc soldier, frail and lame, that lived alone for six months in the tower of Kingsworthy and
robbed the North-Tooks' traps. It wore a cloak of Mirkwood green taken from an Elf of the Woodland
Realm. Makheneb shot it through the spine.

name: The man in the blue-and-white belt
side: foes
first: The ruins, spring 2965
---

A shady man who came to Bree a year and a half before the tale began, asking a great many questions,
and whom Perry's superior at the gate waved through. The Company found his bones in the outer chamber
beneath the ruins, clutching the piece he had broken out of the stone wheel, and his ghost put out
every lantern before Már cleaved it ([[02-findemir-and-the-ruins|Findemir & the Ruins]]). He had
come from the south, and somebody had sent him.

name: Moriel
epithet: steward of the High Watch
side: allies
first: Barad Tarminalë, spring 2965
---

A sturdy woman of the Dúnedain who kept the winter at [[barad-tarminale|Barad Tarminalë]], her first
there, with only three travellers all season and a great many games invented against herself. She
opened the gate to the Company, fed them, learned Lily's card game, and taught Perry that mud is not
the right thing to put in a wound. She tended [[findemir|Findemir]], and trusted Marigold with his
journal and map, though what they held was not shared outside the Dúnedain.

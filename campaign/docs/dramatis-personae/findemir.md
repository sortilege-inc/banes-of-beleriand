A Ranger of the North, with a bushy, curly auburn beard, no moustache, and hair that curls too. He
passed through the Prancing Pony saying he was setting out west, and [[03-lily-goatleaf|Lily]] went
after him. The caretaking of the ruins north of [[barad-tarminale|Barad Tarminalë]] was his to do
this year. He took [[01-mar|Már]] with him from her clan without telling her why, and would not ride.
The frost-wolves brought him down a few hours out, and the Company found him bleeding at Már's feet.
He lived, but could not sit up or speak, and he stayed at the fort in [[moriel|Moriel]]'s care while
the Company finished his errand ([[02-findemir-and-the-ruins|Findemir & the Ruins]]).

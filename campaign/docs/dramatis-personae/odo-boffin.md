first: Michel Delving, summer 2965

A barber-surgeon and animal doctor of Michel Delving, of the healers who say *that's on backwards*
and set it right, or cut it off. There is a hacksaw on his back wall. He set Ammie's leg and looked at
Marigold's foot, and was aghast at the ideas someone had put in the boy's head.

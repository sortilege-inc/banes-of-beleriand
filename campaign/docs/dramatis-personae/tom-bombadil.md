A big man in bright yellow boots and a blue jacket, who sings as he goes and skips stones down the
brooks of the Old Forest. Within the Old Forest his power is complete; the bears go where he tells
them. The Elves call him *Iarwain Ben-adar*, the oldest and fatherless, and [[eryndil|Eryndil]] says
all these lands were Tom's lands when her people first came to Eriador. He and [[goldberry|Goldberry]]
are the Company's patrons, and his house their refuge.

It was Tom who told them where [[findemir|Findemir]] had gone, and that things had been loosed that
needed to be put back to sleep. When [[04-marigold-cotton|Marigold]] showed him her drawing of the
white beast under the ruins he knew it at once, and marked on Perry's map where others of its kind
lie ([[03-the-inner-chamber|The Inner Chamber]]). He met them again on the Brandywine Bridge with a
honeycomb for [[cirdan|Círdan]] that he owed him, and in the end he came to the stables of the
Havens and took Strawberry home.

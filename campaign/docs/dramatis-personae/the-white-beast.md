---
name: The white beast
side: foes
first: The inner chamber, spring 2965
---

A beast a couple of hundred feet long, with white fur like a bear's and huge horns and tusks, shaped
more like a man than a dragon, asleep beneath the glass floor of the inner chamber. It rolled over in
its sleep while the Company stood above it. Marigold drew it in the Rangers' ledger. Tom knew it: one
of several such beings, bound or sleeping in secret places.

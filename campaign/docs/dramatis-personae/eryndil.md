A scholarly Elf of Lindon, of some standing by her clothes and her very fine horse, travelling with
[[celenneth|Celenneth]]'s household and a horse laden with heavy bags. She does not answer
off the top of her head; she waits until camp is made. She gave the Company Tom's elvish name,
*Iarwain Ben-adar*, and told them something of *Melkor's playthings*. Her youngest child is some
three hundred and fifty years old; one has sailed west, and one died at the Battle of Five Armies.

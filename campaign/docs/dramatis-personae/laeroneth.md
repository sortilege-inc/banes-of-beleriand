---
name: Laeroneth
epithet: of the Dúnedain
side: allies
first: The Dwarven House, summer 2965
---

A woman of the Dúnedain, one of three studying at the Arantarion who take it in turns to keep a
corner of the Dwarven House. They too have met things that are not in their proper places. She
offered to help Lily in the library, and suggested where the tale of the Great Bow of Angnir might be
found.

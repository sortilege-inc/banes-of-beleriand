The Shipwright, lord of the Grey Havens,. He heard them out,
cut short *Ghostcleavers* with an apology, and later, when the *Lómelindë* came home without the
*Gwaeloth*, laid his charge on them: the Banes ought in the end to be destroyed, and until then each
must be made safe for every generation to come, by a law or a rite or a story, so long as it is never
broken ([[08-the-returned-ship-and-the-ride-north|The Returned Ship]]). He fears the Beast of Belegaer
has woken in the sea. He sent [[nenuviel|Nenuviel]] north with them, and agreed to hold the next ship
until spring.

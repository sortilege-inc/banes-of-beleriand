The self-styled curator of the [[mathom-house|Mathom-house]] in Michel Delving, and its little
fiefdom's queen. *This is not a library. It is a Mathom-house.* She helped Makheneb splint
[[ammie|Ammie]]'s broken leg with a broomstick and apologised over and over, and meant to make a sign
so that children would not climb the ladder.

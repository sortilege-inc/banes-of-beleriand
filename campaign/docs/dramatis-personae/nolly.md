Már's nephew, one of [[etta|Etta]]'s twin boys, who has begun to learn Már's own trade of laying the
dead to rest, to his mother's unease. He had been missing for weeks. The Company met him charging at
them with the maddened Dwarves, his eyes rolled white, until Már called him by name and he came back
to himself. He remembers nothing but being at home in the mountains.

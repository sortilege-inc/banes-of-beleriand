Tom's wife, a fair-haired woman who welcomes every guest as though she had expected them, and who
often had. She knew [[findemir|Findemir]] at once from Lily's poor description, and bettered it.
She lent [[02-makheneb|Makheneb]] her horse Strawberry, to be brought back within a year, and sent
the Company off with a basket of mushrooms and jars of preserves.

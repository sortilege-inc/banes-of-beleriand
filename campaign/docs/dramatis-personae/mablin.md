name: Mablin
side: allies
first: The walls of the Havens, end of summer 2965
---

A guard on the walls of the Havens with the air of a grandfather and a grandfather's knack for
stories, the *oh, that reminds me* kind. He wants very much to go west, and he does not want to
leave. When the *Lómelindë* came home he told Perry that the death of an Elf is like the death of a
human child, and then said nothing, and they smoked together.

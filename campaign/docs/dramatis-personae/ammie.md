A hobbit child of Michel Delving, and the mind behind the heist of his grandmother's marble bust from
the [[mathom-house|Mathom-house]]. He fixed Marigold's bleeding nose with a sharp tug in the dark.
The next morning his part was to fall from the library ladder and scream; his leg caught in the rungs
and broke in earnest, and Makheneb carried him to [[odo-boffin|Odo Boffin]].

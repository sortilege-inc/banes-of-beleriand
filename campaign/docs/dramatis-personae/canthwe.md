---
name: Canthwë
side: others
first: The Dwarven House, summer 2965
---

An old man with a long beard, a Man by his looks, who walks about the Havens, gardens a little,
smokes, and eats off whatever tray is nearest. He goes where he likes and sees Círdan when he
pleases. Smoking with him, Marigold saw something glorious, and something else she could not quite
remember. Once he named, with sudden clearness, every Elf who had sailed on both ships and whether
each came home; a moment later it was gone. Círdan says he soaked up the curse of a place in the east
the way a rag soaks up wine, and that he once went by another name, which Círdan has sealed.

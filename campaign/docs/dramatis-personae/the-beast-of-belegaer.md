---
name: The Beast of Belegaer
side: foes
first: Círdan's charge, end of summer 2965
---

A thing of the deep sea that Círdan fears has woken. He gave the Company a scrap of script with its
name in the Black Speech. Long ago a Silmaril was lost in the deep, and he fears it may have roused
the creature; if the jewel could be taken from it, perhaps it could be settled again.

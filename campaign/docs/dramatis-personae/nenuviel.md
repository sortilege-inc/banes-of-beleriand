---
name: Nenuviel
epithet: poet of the Havens
side: allies
first: Mithlond, summer 2965
---

A poet of the Havens, first one of the Company's escorts and then Círdan's aide, sent north to judge
the matter of the [[cavity-stone|Cavity Stone]] for him. She knows nothing of mountains and leans on
the Company for the rest. In the valley below the Blue Mountains she fought from horseback and killed
one of the maddened Dwarves gracefully, before a hammer crushed her leg; she walks on a crutch now.

---
name: The frozen man
side: foes
first: The inner chamber, spring 2965
---

A Man, no Ranger, from further south than Tharbad by the Bree-folk's guess, found standing on the great wheel beneath the ruins
with the hilt of a broken noble's sword in his hands, as though he had been trying to prise the stone
open. He was not stone and not dead; he had simply stopped. In his pocket was a passphrase. The
Company carried him out, and neither Makheneb nor Tom could undo what had been done to him.

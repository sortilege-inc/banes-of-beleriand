Már's sister, of the Blue Mountains. She and her husband [[onar|Onar]] grow things in the deep places
of the mountain, food raised without the sun, and she keeps a bakery that makes the most of it: the greatest of
her specialities are her braided beards, jam running through them like ribbons in hair. Her son
[[nolly|Nolly]] had been missing for weeks when Már brought him home.

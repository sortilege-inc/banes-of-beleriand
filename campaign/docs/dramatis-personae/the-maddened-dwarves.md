---
name: The maddened Dwarves
side: foes
first: The valley below the Blue Mountains, end of summer 2965
---

Seven Dwarves of the Blue Mountains who fell on the Company in the valley below the mountains, with
hammers and axes, their eyes rolled back white, fighting like beasts and not like Dwarves. One walked
up a spear to take it from Nenuviel's hands; one bore an open axe-wound in his chest. Three died, two
were brought down alive, [[veig|Veig]] was bound, and [[nolly|Nolly]] came back to himself. Thramli's
party had died at their hands.

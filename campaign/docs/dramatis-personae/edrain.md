---
name: Edrain
epithet: steward of the Dwarven House
side: allies
first: The Grey Havens, summer 2965
---

The steward of the Dwarven House in Nevlond, where the Elves lodge anyone who is not an Elf and has no
host. His standard of dress is a good deal finer than most. It was Edrain who brought word, twice,
that Círdan would see them.

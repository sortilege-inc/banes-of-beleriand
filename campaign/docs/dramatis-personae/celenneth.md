A Ranger of the Dúnedain, travelling to the Grey Havens with her household: [[linnea|Linnea]], her
partner, their children [[brynja|Brynja]] and [[hallas|Hallas]], and the Elf [[eryndil|Eryndil]]. The
two companies travelled together for some days west past Evendim. She had crossed the hills of
Evendim once on her way to the mountains, and she knew a little fable of the [[tree-of-sorrow|Tree of
Sorrow]]. With Eryndil she looked over Makheneb's great bow and judged it Númenórean.

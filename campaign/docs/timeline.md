# Timeline

The Third Age, as the Company has lived it. Each line links to its chapter.

## Spring, 2965

- The Company meets at Tom's house in the Old Forest, and sets out for Barad Tarminalë, ten days'
  journey north ([[01-from-bree-to-toms-house|From Bree, to Tom's House, to Barad Tarminalë]]).
- Frost-wolves bring down Findemir a few hours north of the fort; the Company saves him, and Már
  joins them. Moriel gives Marigold his journal and map. Below the cavern they mend the stone wheel,
  and Már cleaves the ghost of the man in the blue-and-white belt
  ([[02-findemir-and-the-ruins|Findemir & the Ruins]]).
- In the inner chamber they find the frozen man, and the white beast asleep beneath the floor.
  Marigold writes the ledger. Tom marks the other Banes on Perry's map
  ([[03-the-inner-chamber|The Inner Chamber]]).

## Early summer, 2965

- West with Celenneth's household past Evendim; the Company parts from them and turns for the North
  Moors. The long-folk of the Tree of Sorrow; the Orc of Long Cleeve; Kingsworthy
  ([[04-the-north-moors|The North Moors]]).
- Into the Tree of Sorrow, and the sleeper in the grotto beneath it. Frogmorton, and Marigold's news:
  she is to be a grandmother. Tom on the Brandywine Bridge
  ([[05-beneath-the-tree-of-sorrow|Beneath the Tree of Sorrow]]).
- Michel Delving and the Mathom-house; the heist of the bust; the troll of the bridge; the Grey Havens
  ([[06-the-mathom-house-and-the-troll|The Mathom-house and the Troll]]).

## Mid-to-late summer, 2965

- A summer in Mithlond: the Arantarion and its forges, Canthwë, and a first audience with Círdan,
  pressed by the news of a westbound ship turned back on the Straight Road ([[07-mithlond|Mithlond]]).

## End of summer, 2965

- Mereth Bantiel. The *Lómelindë* comes home with a third of those who sailed in her, and the
  *Gwaeloth* not at all. Círdan's charge, and the ride north with Nenuviel
  ([[08-the-returned-ship-and-the-ride-north|The Returned Ship, and the Ride North]]).
- Thramli's party found dead in the valley below the Blue Mountains; the maddened Dwarves; Nolly
  called home. The mourning of the dead, and the council waiting
  ([[09-the-blue-mountains|The Blue Mountains]]).

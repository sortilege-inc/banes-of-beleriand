---
name: The Tree of Sorrow
region: The North Moors
---

A great tree in the North Moors, half a day north of [[long-cleeve|Long Cleeve]], made, the fable says,
of many trees grown together to shelter from some threat. Beside it a hill is crowned with six standing
stones. The long-folk have built a whole township in and around it, with stairs of roots curling up
inside; beneath it are passages lit by glowing growths, a well of bitter water that makes the heart
race, and, down a passage the long-folk will not take, a grotto about a pool where
[[the-sleeper-beneath-the-tree|the sleeper]] lies ([[05-beneath-the-tree-of-sorrow|Beneath the Tree of
Sorrow]]).

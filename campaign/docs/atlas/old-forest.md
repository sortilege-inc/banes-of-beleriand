---
name: The Old Forest
region: Bree-land
---

Tom's land, east of the Brandywine: older than any wood of the Shire, thick with the smell of loam and
leaf-rot and wet bark, and full of mushrooms Marigold had never seen. Bears walk there, and Tom sends
them away with a song. The Company first met at Tom's cottage, in a garden where nothing is fenced or
set in rows and the beans zigzag up to the door, and where freeze-berries come up for a few weeks
after the last snow ([[01-from-bree-to-toms-house|From Bree, to Tom's House]]).

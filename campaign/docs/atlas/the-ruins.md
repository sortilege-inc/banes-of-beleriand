---
name: The ruins beneath the cavern
region: The North
---

A Númenórean place a day and a half north-east of [[barad-tarminale|Barad Tarminalë]], in a bowl among
the hills, where the Rangers do a caretaking every few decades. A wolf-den lies in the cavern mouth; an
hour's slow descent brings you to a great chamber with a wheel of stone carved with a ward, and a pair
of doors faced in filigree beyond it. A wide stair goes down to an eight-sided hall some two hundred and
fifty feet across, with a greater wheel at its centre, three side rooms (a sleeping room, a reading
room with the Rangers' ledger, and a store of relics), and a floor like frosted glass. Beneath the
glass sleeps [[the-white-beast|the white beast]] ([[03-the-inner-chamber|The Inner Chamber]]).

Ered Luin, the mountains west of the Shire, where Már's kin live in tunnels and little rooms and coves
cut into the rock, raising food in the deep places. A weigh station stands on the way up; above it the
Hall, where the council of the Dwarves of the Blue Mountains sits, seven when all are present. A valley
half a day below the nearest hall is where [[thramli|Thramli]]'s party died
([[09-the-blue-mountains|The Blue Mountains]]).

---
name: The Mathom-house
region: The Shire
---

A museum in [[michel-delving|Michel Delving]], famous for its cookbooks and family trees, its hobbit-lore,
its old weapons and maps. Its book-room is all narrow gaps between shelves, with books piled three feet
high on the floor. It is [[malva-slowfoot|Malva Slowfoot]]'s fiefdom, guarded by
[[firework|Firework]]. Perry copied its flower map there; Marigold went out of it with a marble bust and
came back for the sixth volume of Melody Took's journals ([[06-the-mathom-house-and-the-troll|The
Mathom-house and the Troll]]).

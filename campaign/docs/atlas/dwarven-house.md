---
name: The Dwarven House
region: Lindon
---

A single long building filling a city block just inside Nevlond's main gate, with balconies all round
its upper floor. Despite its name it is not only for Dwarves: it is where the Elves lodge anyone who is
not an Elf and has no host, as a kindness. [[edrain|Edrain]] keeps it.

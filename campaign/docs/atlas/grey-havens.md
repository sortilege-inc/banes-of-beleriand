---
name: The Grey Havens
region: Lindon
---

Mithlond, the Havens of the Elves, on either side of the Firth of Lhûn where it opens into the Gulf and
the Gulf into the sea: Nevlond on the eastern shore, green with gardens, and Haelond on the western, all
fine stonework, joined by a bridge with a gate. Each city has a tower; Nevlond's is a lighthouse. Tall
grey trees scent Haelond with their resin. Here [[cirdan|Círdan]] builds his ships, and here the
Company spent the summer, first at the [[dwarven-house|Dwarven House]] and then in a villa of their own
([[07-mithlond|Mithlond]]).

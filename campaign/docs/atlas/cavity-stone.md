---
name: The Cavity Stone
region: The Blue Mountains
---

A very old thing high in the Blue Mountains, as [[thramli|Thramli]] told it. It was not made by Dwarves;
it was found. A group of Dwarves keeps the place where it lies, left as it was and tended, the way a
Dwarf-hall keeps a part of itself as it always was. It has been doing strange things to the minds of
those who keep it, and it took the Dwarves thirty years to see that it was what all their troubles had
in common. [[nenuviel|Nenuviel]] has come to judge it for Círdan.

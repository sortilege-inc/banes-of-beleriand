---
name: Barad Tarminalë
region: The North
---

A Rangers' way-station in the hills north beyond Fornost, ten days' journey from Tom's house: a ring of
stone nearly cut into the hills, lying mostly on their northern and western faces, so that from the
south it might never be seen. Its gates are great stakes of timber that open inward. Inside are cabins
with bare beds and cupboards mostly empty. [[moriel|Moriel]] kept the winter there, and tended
[[findemir|Findemir]] there after the wolves. The Company rested there after the ruins, and there Tom
found them.

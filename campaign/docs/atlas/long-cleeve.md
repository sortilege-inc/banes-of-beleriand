---
name: Long Cleeve
region: The North Moors
---

A little village on the edge of the Northfarthing, a handful of houses on either side of the Water where
it runs down out of the Moors. It is the home of the North-Tooks, who claim descent from the Bullroarer;
their cousins further south dispute it, over drinking contests and conkers and golf at the family
reunions. [[fosco-north-took|Fosco]] and [[peony-north-took|Peony]] gave the Company beds there, and a
stable.

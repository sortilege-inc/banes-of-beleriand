name: Bree
region: Bree-land
---

The chief town of Bree-land, on the East Road, where Lily waited tables at the Prancing Pony and Perry
kept the gate. It is a small island with enemies all round it that no one else can see, as the
Rangers told Lily. Lately the folk coming through have grown stranger and more unsavoury, and they are
gathering there. The man in the blue-and-white belt got in by a back way.

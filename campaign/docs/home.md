# Banes of Beleriand

*The One Ring*, in the years between *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings*. The tale begins in the
spring of 2965 of the Third Age.

Things have been loosed in the North that need to be put back to sleep. In the Old Forest, Tom
Bombadil set a hobbit, an Elf of Mirkwood and two folk of Bree on the trail of a Ranger who had gone
looking for help; on the road they picked up a Dwarf who had been sent north without being told why.
Under the ruins of the North they found the first of the Banes, sleeping beneath a floor of glass. Tom
marked the others on their map. At the Grey Havens, Círdan laid his charge on them: the Banes cannot
yet be destroyed, but each can be made safe, for this generation and every generation after, so long
as the way of keeping it is never broken.

The Company has gone north with Círdan's aide to the Blue Mountains, and to the Cavity
Stone.
